Model predictive control (MPC) of a grid-connected service area equipped with an
electric energy storage system and fast-charging stations for plug-in electric
vehicles. The controller manages the storage to lower and flatten the power drawn
from the grid across different microgrid connection schemes.

**Topics:** MPC · energy storage · smart grids · EV charging
**Tools:** MATLAB / Simulink · Python
